Analytical Findings

Key Insights from Our 8 Worksheets

Every chart was validated step-by-step in Tableau Public to uncover macro and micro patterns in Olympic sports performance:

1

Country Dominance & Medal Breakdown

United States (735 total medals: 250 Gold, 208 Silver, 277 Bronze) and China (696 total medals: 230 Gold, 230 Silver, 236 Bronze) lead overall standings, followed by Russia (429) and Great Britain (401).

2

Top Sports by Total & Gold Medals

Skateboarding, Gymnastics, and Fencing top both overall medal volume (6,025 each) and Gold medal counts (1,959 Golds each), closely followed by Judo, Weightlifting, and Volleyball.

3

Host Country Advantage & Medal Efficiency

Japan (105), United States (100), China (98), and Italy (93) record the highest host country performance counts, while Iran (1,032.1), Latvia (1,015.4), and Poland (986.3) top cumulative Medal Efficiency Pct.
